What type of asset is it?

We currently finance operating assets in Egypt in these three sectors.

Before you start, have these documents ready to upload (PDF, Word, Excel or clear photos, up to 10 MB each):
  • Commercial register extract
  • Signed contract with your buyer
  • 12 to 24 months of bank statements showing the buyer’s payments
  • Commissioning certificate
If you have them, also: the buyer’s financial statements, a production report, your insurance certificate, your company’s financial statements, management CVs and tax clearance, a legal opinion or title document, and a valuation report. If a document is not ready yet, you can send it within 30 days from your dashboard.
The profile has 7 short steps and takes about 20 minutes. You can save and continue later at any time.
