Asset-Backed Exposure to Contracted Clean Energy Revenue.

Renewables Vault provides investors access to asset-backed exposure to contracted renewable energy revenue — transactions structured around specific, operating clean energy assets, not projected cash flows.

Each transaction is underwritten through RARE™, Renewables Vault's proprietary risk methodology, and structured for a defined term. Current origination is focused in Egypt, across three clean energy asset classes: biofuel and biodiesel, solar C&I, and circular economy infrastructure.

Transaction-level detail — including structure, terms, and underlying off-takers — is shared only with registered investors Renewables Vault has engaged with directly.
